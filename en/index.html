Building legal observables. From beauty to justice

Computational methods for the empirical analysis of the qualities and effects of legal rules.

Master's thesis in Law · University of Sannio · academic year 2025/2026 · Antonella Ninfadoro · supervisor Prof. Nicola Lettieri

In brief

Can we observe whether a law keeps its promise?

The thesis uses the methods of computational social science to make the qualities and effects of legal rules observable. It tests them on a single article: art. 33 of Law 104/1992, three days of paid leave a month to care for a family member with a severe disability. In 2024, 608,206 private-sector employees used it (INPS).

The starting point

A law promises a protection. Can we observe whether it keeps that promise?

The framework observes the distance between what the law recognises and what people manage to obtain.

Approfondisci

Article 3(2) of the Italian Constitution requires the removal of obstacles that limit equality "in fact": attention shifts from the text of the rule to people's lives. The framework is not a single method. Each technique comes in after a precise question.

Level 1 · The provision

First we read the text, with tools that count and recognise

The text is measured: readability, structure, cross-references, terminology, formal regularity.

Approfondisci

A legal text is made of words, and words can be counted and recognised. The three names for the condition correspond to layers of amendments: "disabilità" appears only in the paragraphs rewritten in 2022.

Then we look at where the rule sits, in the network of other rules

The cross-references found in the text become the edges of a network.

A provision that is clear on its own can become obscure inside a chain of cross-references and amendments. Art. 33 refers to 4 other acts. With its update notes the corpus reaches 20 acts: amendments, interpretations, derogations and a Constitutional Court ruling. Ten relations are already verified on the text.

Level 3 · Real effects

Then we ask who actually manages to use the protection

We leave the text and enter the data on recipients.

445,167 → 608,206private-sector employees using the leave, 2020 → 2024 (INPS, as cited in the thesis)
?people who meet the requirements but do not use the protection: the missing figure
Approfondisci

Attributing an effect to a rule requires an adequate research design. The observable to build is non-take-up: who meets the requirements and does not use the protection, where and why. In 2020 more leave days were available (Decree-Law 18/2020), so the comparison with 2024 needs care.

Level 4 · Possible scenarios

Finally we explore what cannot be observed

Simulation builds possible worlds: other rules, other timings, other information.

  1. Knowthe right
  2. Obtainthe assessment
  3. Submitthe application
  4. Usethe leave
Approfondisci

Results depend on the assumptions and must be read with sensitivity analysis. The third prototype simulates 1,000 hypothetical entitled people going through the four steps; each scenario is repeated 100 times.

Hypothetical numbers. Illustrative experiment, not calibrated on real data: it shows which data to collect.

From beauty to justice

Each level incorporates the previous ones, and evaluation grows with them

Beauty and justice are the poles of an evaluative continuum, not scores to be computed.

BeautyJustice
ComprehensibilityCoherenceAccessibilityEquity
Approfondisci

The first two levels mainly inform a judgement on formal quality; the third and fourth a judgement on effects. Data do not say that a law is just. They make observable the consequences that matter for that judgement, which remains entrusted to an explicit normative criterion.

What was done, what emerged

1 · the provision 45.3

Gulpease index of art. 33 (Italian text): hard for readers with lower-secondary education. The paragraph granting the right has a 110-word sentence.

Operations: paragraphs, sentences, readability, cross-references, vocabulary, 7 drafting rules.

It does not say whether the rule is just or works.

 2 · the relations 10 · 9

Interventions on art. 33 verified on the text, over thirty years; nine affect paragraph 3. References to repealed laws from 2001 to 2022.

Operations: 20 acts collected from Normattiva, relations classified and verified, comparison of the 2000, 2010 and 2022 versions.

More links do not mean a worse law.

 3 · protection in practice 44.6% → 59.8%

Hypothetical people who reach the leave if the informed share rises from 40% to 90%. With equal assessment times, the gap between areas disappears.

Operations: INPS data on users, simulation of 1,000 entitled people repeated 100 times, sensitivity analysis.

Hypothetical numbers: the figure on entitled non-users is missing.

All prototypes are proofs of feasibility: they show that the procedures work on the actual text, not that they produce validated empirical knowledge. Computation adds tools to legal analysis without replacing it.
