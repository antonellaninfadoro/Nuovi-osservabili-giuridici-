Building legal observables. From beauty to justice

Computational methods for the empirical analysis of the qualities and effects of legal rules. A four-level framework, tested on Italian Law no. 104 of 5 February 1992.

Master's thesis in Law · University of Sannio · academic year 2025/2026 · Antonella Ninfadoro · supervisor Prof. Nicola Lettieri

The thesis in brief

The site and the thesis

This site accompanies the master's thesis Costruire osservabili giuridici (Building legal observables) and shows its applied part in practice. To get oriented, here is briefly what it is about.

What

Information technologies and computational social science now make observable aspects of reality that were previously beyond empirical inquiry. The thesis asks whether, and to what extent, these concepts and methods can contribute to the study of the quality of law.

Why

Looking at the formal properties of texts, such as clarity, structure and relations between rules, is not enough. We need to reach substance: the effects rules produce and the distance between the protection they promise and the protection they actually provide.

Thesis, Abstract, p. 10, and Introduction, pp. 7-10

How we get there

  1. Ch. I Computational social science

    Computation extends what we can observe, as the microscope extended sight: Humphreys' "empirical extension".

    Thesis, pp. 14-16
  2. Ch. II Computational legal empiricism

    Law is not made of texts alone: it includes relations between sources, behaviours, decisions. Computation makes them observable and, through simulation, explores possible worlds.

    Thesis, pp. 53, 59-60, 68
  3. Ch. III The qualities of law

    Clarity, simplicity, coherence, stability, effectiveness and accessibility are studied separately. The thesis orders them in four levels, from text to effects, from beauty to justice.

    Thesis, pp. 72-125
  4. Ch. IV · here The test

    The framework is tested on Italian Law no. 104 of 5 February 1992, with three prototypes that can be used and checked.

    Thesis, pp. 119-121

In the first three chapters the thesis builds the theoretical framework. The fourth tests it on a concrete case: that is what this site shows.

  1. 1 · The test bed

    We test the framework on a whole statute: Law 104/1992.

    It is not only about health care: it also covers assistance, social integration, education and work. This breadth makes it suitable for comparing, in each area, what the law promises with what people actually obtain.

    disability assessmentassistancesocial integrationeducationwork
    Thesis, ch. IV, §§ 1.1-1.2, p. 119
  2. 2 · The example

    Within the law we take one article: art. 33, the paid work leave for caring for a family member with a severe disability.

    It is short enough to analyse paragraph by paragraph, it has many cross-references and amendments, and there are real data on who uses it: 608,206 private-sector employees in 2024 (INPS, as cited in the thesis).

    Thesis, ch. IV, § 1.2, p. 119
3 · The aim of the thesis

The construction of legal observables: using computational tools to make the qualities and effects of a law observable, from the text that grants a protection to the people who actually manage to use it.

Law 104 raises the question of the distance between the formal recognition of a protection and the concrete possibility of benefiting from it. This is the problem the thesis addresses in the transition from formal quality to substantive quality and to justice.

Three linked tools, on the work leave of art. 33

1The provision
QuestionWhat can be measured in the text of art. 33?
What it doesIt analyses an authentic provision of Law 104 and identifies cross-references, linguistic structures, recurring formulas and some basic readability indicators. It is an actual computational result, though a limited one.
ShowsA program recognises paragraphs, cross-references, vocabulary and readability in the real text, with results checked by hand. Example: the same condition is named in 3 ways.
Does not showThat the rule is badly written or unjust.
2The relations
QuestionWhich other rules does the protection depend on?
What it doesIt collects the provisions linked to the leave, with a small set of relations verified before being shown. It shows how the unit of observation expands from the single provision to the system it belongs to. The navigable network will be drawn once the method sheets are approved.
ShowsLinks can be collected and classified in a checkable way: 20 acts, 10 relations already verified on the text.
Does not showThat more links mean a worse law.
3Protection in practice
QuestionWhat might the gap between those entitled and those who use the leave depend on?
What it doesIt imagines a hypothetical population of entitled people. By changing a few parameters (information barriers, administrative delays, territorial differences) it shows how the share of people who actually access the protection changes.
ShowsA simulation with more or less informed people makes the assumptions explicit and shows which step weighs most: in the model, information.
Does not showHow many people are actually left out: the numbers are hypothetical.

Rigour on the third tool. Without empirical data and a validated model, the simulation does not represent the real effects of Law 104: it is an illustrative computational experiment based on explicit assumptions.

A procedure that works is not yet validated empirical knowledge. The prototypes achieve the first and prepare the ground for the second (thesis, ch. IV, § 2, p. 121).

Three key concepts

What

An observable is an aspect of a phenomenon that can be detected, measured and analysed systematically.

The thesis applies this idea to law, which is made of texts, of relations between rules and of institutional practices.

Thesis, ch. I, § 1, p. 14, and Introduction, p. 7
Why

The quality of legislation is studied along separate dimensions: clarity, simplicity, coherence, stability, effectiveness, accessibility.

These dimensions say a lot about how a rule is written, but little about who actually receives its benefits. For art. 33 this is exactly the missing figure: how many people would be entitled to the leave without managing to obtain it.

Thesis, ch. III, § 1.3, pp. 96-97; ch. IV, § 1.2, p. 120
What the thesis adds

A single framework linking text, system, effects and scenarios, with an evaluative criterion that runs from beauty to justice.

  • Question first, tool second: each technique comes into play only after a precise question about the legal phenomenon.
  • Verifiable prototypes on Law 104/1992: they show that the procedures work on the real text.
  • New observables for art. 33: cross-references between paragraphs, layers of vocabulary, number of steps needed to know whether one is entitled.
Thesis, ch. III, § 2, p. 97, and ch. IV, § 2, p. 121. The new observables are original proposals [P], to be submitted to the supervisor.

Computation is understood "as a quid pluris and never as a minus": it does not replace legal analysis, it extends it (thesis, Introduction, p. 9; our translation).

The starting point

A law promises a protection. Can we observe whether it keeps that promise?

The framework observes the distance between what the law recognises and what people manage to obtain.

Article 3(2) of the Italian Constitution requires the removal of obstacles that limit equality "in fact". The phrase shifts attention from the text of the rule to people's lives. Computation offers tools to observe the distance between what the law recognises and what people manage to obtain.

The framework is not a single method. It relates four elements: a legal object, a question of knowledge, an operation that turns the object into data, and a technique that makes a property of those data visible.

Level 3 · Real effects

Then we ask who actually manages to use the protection

We leave the text and enter the data on recipients.

445,167 → 608,206private-sector employees using the leave, 2020 → 2024 (INPS, as cited in the thesis)
?people who meet the requirements but do not use the protection: the missing figure
Approfondisci

The third level leaves the text and enters the data on recipients. Attributing an effect to a rule requires an adequate research design. The first step is to build the right observable.

Private-sector employees using the leave rose from 445,167 in 2020 to 608,206 in 2024 (INPS, as cited in the thesis). The most important figure is missing: how many people would be entitled to the leave without managing to obtain it. The observable to build is non-take-up: who meets the requirements and does not use the protection, where and why.

Official data, few. The available data and the missing figure are gathered in the third prototype, together with the level-four simulation.

Level 4 · Possible scenarios

Finally we explore what cannot be observed

Simulation builds possible worlds: other rules, other timings, other information.

  1. Knowthe right
  2. Obtainthe assessment
  3. Submitthe application
  4. Usethe leave
Approfondisci

Simulation builds possible worlds: what would happen with other rules, other timings, other information. Its results depend on the assumptions and must be read with sensitivity analysis. It extends observation towards the possible; it does not replace it.

For art. 33 one could simulate a hypothetical population of entitled people going through the steps reconstructed in the first prototype: knowing the right, obtaining the assessment, submitting the application, using the leave. Varying timings and information would show which step weighs most.

44.6% → 59.8%people who end up using the leave, if the informed share rises from 40% to 90% (mean of 100 runs)
37.8% → 51.7%in the area with slower assessments, if timings become equal

Hypothetical numbers. Outputs of an illustrative simulation with 1,000 people, not calibrated on real data. They indicate which data to collect: how many people know the right, assessment times by area, obstacles at work. A simulation is an illustrative experiment based on explicit assumptions, not a representation of real effects.

From beauty to justice

Each level incorporates the previous ones, and evaluation grows with them

Beauty and justice are the poles of an evaluative continuum, not scores to be computed.

BeautyJustice
ComprehensibilityCoherenceAccessibilityEquity
Approfondisci

Beauty and justice are the poles of an evaluative continuum, not scores to be computed. The first two levels mainly inform a judgement on formal quality; the third and fourth a judgement on effects. Data do not say that a law is just. They make observable the consequences that matter for that judgement, which remains entrusted to an explicit normative criterion.

Three prototypes for four levels

The thesis plans three prototypes, and the site presents all of them. The first two work on the actual text; the third gathers the available official data and runs an illustrative simulation experiment with declared assumptions.
